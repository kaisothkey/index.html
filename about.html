About

My work is influenced by current interests I have or memories I want to cherish. I have been strictly doing traditional media for quite some time with the exception of digital painting when I’m limited on resources. I find myself enjoying the physicality of drawing, crafting and painting. My process involves finding anything I can get my hands on and “trusting the process.”


Although most of my work is traditional I am open to trying all sorts of media at least once! I think because I haven’t experienced much as an artist I do hope in the future I can say that I’ve mastered certain mediums. I think what I want viewers to notice about my work is to feel a sense of relatability while also being able to say they can repurpose my art and make it their own. I say this because as an artist I take a lot of my inspiration from other artists’ and their work.


Kai Ke was born and raised in Socal, Long Beach. Graduated from Long Beach City College with an Associate Degree for Transfer in June 2026. They are currently attending San Jose State University and majoring in Studio Art Practice. They’re currently exploring new media art and the digital world. Their main focus is self expression and creating art has been a gateway for them. Being able to create work and find community through it is what keeps them going.
